== Requirements Class "Research Object"

[[research-object-overview]]
=== Overview

The link:https://www.researchobject.org/ro-crate/specification[Research Object Crate (RO-Crate)]
is a lightweight approach to packaging research data with their metadata and provenance information.
The RO-Crate specification defines a simple, structured way to describe research outputs, including datasets,
software, and other digital artifacts, along with their associated metadata.

This requirement expands upon the <<OAProc-1>> job results (i.e., `/jobs/{jobID}/results`) with content negotiation
and definitions allowing the retrieval of a _Research Object_ bundle from a completed job, which simultaneously includes
the job results and the <<rc_provenance>> details produced by the process execution.
